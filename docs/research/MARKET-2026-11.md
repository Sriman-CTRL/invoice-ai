## 2. Is "files unlock only after payment" unique?

**No, the idea exists in several places.** From the first pass: [FileDue](https://www.goodfirms.co/software/filedue) (Stripe, 2% after 3 free deliveries),
[FileCheckout](https://www.producthunt.com/p/filecheckout/filecheckout) (client pays the freelancer directly),
[Sentpaid](https://alternativeto.net/software/sentpaid/about) (PayPal), [Invoice Ninja Enterprise](https://invoiceninja.com/payment-gateway/)
(document locking). Added in the second pass (search results, vendor help pages not all opened, so "reported"):

- **Photography and delivery tools do gate downloads.** [WeTransfer](https://wetransfer.com/resources/request-payment-for-content/photographers-photos)
  lets a photographer price a transfer and keep the files locked until the payment goes through; [Aryeo](https://support.aryeo.com/en/articles/5947032-content-locking-settings)
  has a setting that locks downloads by default until orders are paid. For Pixieset, no source confirmed locking downloads until
  an invoice is paid (its help pages describe selling digital downloads and download PINs instead). Other gallery tools
  ([PhotoBiz](https://support.photobiz.com/client-galleries/blog1/how-can-i-use-client-galleries-and-invoicing-to-sell-digital-files-online),
  [Pictures Pro](https://www.picturespro.com/support-forum/sytist/47170-cant-get-paid-access-to-work)) say files must be released by hand after an invoice is paid.
- **HoneyBook:** a 2024 community thread says there was no automatic way to hold files until final payment; unconfirmed for 2026.
  **Gumroad** delivers files automatically after payment (that is its sale model, not an invoicing feature). **Dubsado:** nothing found.
  **Bonsai:** its pricing page lists "Lock Attachments" with no description, so it may exist; not confirmed as payment-linked.
- **Escrow services in India** were not researched in this pass (no reliable source was found); do not claim "no escrow competitor".

Conclusion: not unique as an idea. What I still did not find is **a pay-to-unlock flow built on UPI/bank transfer paid straight to an
Indian seller, with UTR proof and GST invoicing**. The honest pitch is that combination, never "first". Absence of search hits is not proof.

## 2b. Evidence of the pain (late payments)

Figures only from these sources; none is a survey of Indian freelancers specifically, and I did not find one:

- Remote's contractor report (global): **85%** of freelancers have invoices paid late at least some of the time
  ([Remote](https://remote.com/en-ph/blog/contractor-management/reversing-late-payment-culture)). Global, not India.
- Recordent's Indian SME receivables report: Indian MSMEs wait **73 days on average** for invoice payments, from about 1.1 lakh MSMEs
  ([MediaBrief](https://mediabrief.com/recordent-report-highlights-working-capital-stress-for-indian-smes-as-average-overdue-receivables-cross-%E2%82%B93-83-crore/),
  [Telangana Today](https://telanganatoday.com/indian-msmes-face-mounting-delayed-payments-recordent-report-reveals)). MSMEs, not freelancers.
- Atradius payment practices barometer, India 2024: reports a negative trend in B2B payment behaviour in India
  ([Atradius](https://atradius.us/reports/payment-practices-barometer-b2b-payment-practices-trends-india-2024.html)); the snippet gave no figure.
- Qualitative: a news item on Indian micro and nano creators describes late payments and unstable income
  ([NewsBytes](https://www.newsbytesapp.com/news/business/indian-micro-and-nano-creators-face-late-payments-unstable-income/tldr)),
  and freelancer-guide articles say many freelancers fear damaging work relationships if they chase
  ([Razorpay learn](https://razorpay.com/learn/scope-and-challenges-of-freelancers/), [YourStory](https://yourstory.com/mystory/these-are-the-major-challenges-faced-by-indian-fre-wibm4b941l)).
  I read only search snippets of these, not the full pages.
- Comparison: a UK study found 35% of self-employed people were not paid on time in the last 12 months

Use "Indian MSMEs wait 73 days on average (Recordent)" and "85% of freelancers worldwide are paid late at least sometimes (Remote)"

## 3. Facts that constrain the product

- **UPI limits.** Per [Paytm](https://paytm.com/blog/news/upi-higher-transaction-limits-sep-2025) and
  [AIR](https://www.newsonair.gov.in/npci-raises-upi-person-to-merchant-transaction-limit-to-%E2%82%B95-lakh), from 15 Sep 2025 P2M
  caps rose to ₹5 lakh per transaction / ₹10 lakh per day for verified merchants in listed categories; P2P stayed at ₹1 lakh/day.
  Many general guides still say ₹1 lakh. Paivo payments go to a seller's personal or business UPI ID, so a ₹1 lakh limit may
  apply to large invoices: the pay page should mention bank transfer (NEFT/RTGS) for big amounts. Confirm on
  [NPCI circulars](https://npci.org.in/what-we-do/upi/circular).
- **Payment-aggregator licensing.** RBI's [Master Direction (Sep 2025)](https://rbi.org.in/scriptS/BS_ViewMasDirections.aspx?id=12896)
  ties authorisation to **handling and settling funds**; a gateway that does not handle funds is defined separately. Paivo
  never holds funds, which supports our position, but I could not find an RBI statement on direct UPI payments to a seller.
  Get a lawyer's opinion before saying "no licence needed" anywhere public.
- **GST e-invoicing.** Still mandatory above ₹5 crore aggregate turnover (Notification 10/2023-CT) per
  [Gimbooks](https://www.gimbooks.com/blog/5-crore-e-invoice-turnover-rule-2026/amp/) and [Busy](https://busy.in/gst/e-invoicing-turnover-limit-vs-gst-threshold-whats-the-difference-and-why-it-matters/);
  a cut to ₹3 or ₹2 crore was discussed but not notified as of mid-2026. Our users (freelancers/small studios) are mostly
  below it, so e-invoicing (IRN/QR) is not needed now. Verify on CBIC.
- **DPDP.** Rules notified 13 Nov 2025; consent-manager rules from about Nov 2026; the main obligations for data
  fiduciaries from about May 2027 ([Mondaq](https://www.mondaq.com/india/data-protection/1708434/operationalising-indias-data-protection-regime-analysing-the-newly-notified-dpdp-rules-2025),
  [Legal500](https://www.legal500.com/firms/237811-saga-legal/c-india/news-and-developments/the-privacy-timer-starts-now-analysing-the-dpdp-rules-and-its-18-month-countdown)).
  Exact dates differ by a day between sources. Paivo's privacy page and erasure/export features already point the right way;
  plan a review before May 2027.

## 4. What is truly differentiated vs table stakes

- **Table stakes:** GST invoices, PDF, reminders, payment links/QR, client portal, recurring invoices. Zoho is free.
- **Differentiated (reported, unverified as unique):** UPI-direct with no gateway fee **plus** pay-to-unlock deliverables **plus**
  UTR proof-and-confirm, for India. The honest pitch is the combination, never "first".
- **Weak spot:** confirmation is manual. Gateway products confirm automatically.

## 5. Threats

1. Zoho Invoice is free and trusted. 2. Razorpay/Cashfree links confirm payments automatically (our manual UTR step is
slower). 3. Any invoicing tool can bolt on "lock files" cheaply. 4. UPI limit/NPCI rule changes. 5. Trust: a new brand asking
people to put client data in it.

## 6. Pricing benchmarks

Zoho Invoice ₹0; Kwill free tier; gateways ~2% per transaction (+₹3 Instamojo, +18% GST on fees); FileDue 2% after free
tier. Paivo's ₹0 and zero-fee UPI are competitive; Pro ₹799 sits well above a free Zoho, which supports the existing note in
PROJECT-HISTORY to reconsider ₹399-499 until automation lands (not changed here).
