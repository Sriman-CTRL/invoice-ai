## 2. Is "files unlock only after payment" unique?

**No, the idea exists.** Reported tools: [FileDue](https://www.goodfirms.co/software/filedue) (Stripe, 2% after 3 free deliveries),
[FileCheckout](https://www.producthunt.com/p/filecheckout/filecheckout) (client pays freelancer directly),
[Sentpaid](https://alternativeto.net/software/sentpaid/about) (PayPal), and
[Invoice Ninja Enterprise](https://invoiceninja.com/payment-gateway/) (document locking). Stripe's own freelancer guide
recommends delivering final work after payment for new clients ([Stripe](https://stripe.com/in/resources/more/how-to-invoice-as-a-freelancer)).
What I did **not** find: one that does it with **UPI/bank transfer paid straight to an Indian seller, with UTR proof and GST
invoicing in one flow**. That combination looks open, but absence of search hits is not proof. Bonsai, Dubsado, Gumroad and
escrow services were not covered; check before claiming "first".

## 3. Facts that constrain the product

- **UPI limits.** Per [Paytm](https://paytm.com/blog/news/upi-higher-transaction-limits-sep-2025) and
  [AIR](https://www.newsonair.gov.in/npci-raises-upi-person-to-merchant-transaction-limit-to-%E2%82%B95-lakh), from 15 Sep 2025 P2M
  caps rose to ₹5 lakh per transaction / ₹10 lakh per day for verified merchants in listed categories; P2P stayed at ₹1 lakh/day.
  Many general guides still say ₹1 lakh. Paivo payments go to a seller's personal or business UPI ID, so a ₹1 lakh limit may
  apply to large invoices: the pay page should mention bank transfer (NEFT/RTGS) for big amounts. Confirm on
  [NPCI circulars](https://npci.org.in/what-we-do/upi/circular).
- **Payment-aggregator licensing.** RBI's [Master Direction (Sep 2025)](https://rbi.org.in/scriptS/BS_ViewMasDirections.aspx?id=12896)
  ties authorisation to **handling and settling funds**; a gateway that does not handle funds is defined separately. Paivo
  never holds funds, which supports our position, but I could not find an RBI statement on direct UPI payments to a seller.
  Get a lawyer's opinion before saying "no licence needed" anywhere public.
- **GST e-invoicing.** Still mandatory above ₹5 crore aggregate turnover (Notification 10/2023-CT) per
  [Gimbooks](https://www.gimbooks.com/blog/5-crore-e-invoice-turnover-rule-2026/amp/) and [Busy](https://busy.in/gst/e-invoicing-turnover-limit-vs-gst-threshold-whats-the-difference-and-why-it-matters/);
  a cut to ₹3 or ₹2 crore was discussed but not notified as of mid-2026. Our users (freelancers/small studios) are mostly
  below it, so e-invoicing (IRN/QR) is not needed now. Verify on CBIC.
- **DPDP.** Rules notified 13 Nov 2025; consent-manager rules from about Nov 2026; the main obligations for data
  fiduciaries from about May 2027 ([Mondaq](https://www.mondaq.com/india/data-protection/1708434/operationalising-indias-data-protection-regime-analysing-the-newly-notified-dpdp-rules-2025),
  [Legal500](https://www.legal500.com/firms/237811-saga-legal/c-india/news-and-developments/the-privacy-timer-starts-now-analysing-the-dpdp-rules-and-its-18-month-countdown)).
  Exact dates differ by a day between sources. Paivo's privacy page and erasure/export features already point the right way;
  plan a review before May 2027.

## 4. What is truly differentiated vs table stakes

- **Table stakes:** GST invoices, PDF, reminders, payment links/QR, client portal, recurring invoices. Zoho is free.
- **Differentiated (reported, unverified as unique):** UPI-direct with no gateway fee **plus** pay-to-unlock deliverables **plus**
  UTR proof-and-confirm, for India. The honest pitch is the combination, never "first".
- **Weak spot:** confirmation is manual. Gateway products confirm automatically.

## 5. Threats

1. Zoho Invoice is free and trusted. 2. Razorpay/Cashfree links confirm payments automatically (our manual UTR step is
slower). 3. Any invoicing tool can bolt on "lock files" cheaply. 4. UPI limit/NPCI rule changes. 5. Trust: a new brand asking
people to put client data in it.

## 6. Pricing benchmarks

Zoho Invoice ₹0; Kwill free tier; gateways ~2% per transaction (+₹3 Instamojo, +18% GST on fees); FileDue 2% after free
tier. Paivo's ₹0 and zero-fee UPI are competitive; Pro ₹799 sits well above a free Zoho, which supports the existing note in
PROJECT-HISTORY to reconsider ₹399-499 until automation lands (not changed here).
