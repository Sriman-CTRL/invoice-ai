# Messages (copy, edit, send one by one)

Rules: send each message **personally**, never in bulk. Say who you are. Fill every `{slot}` with something real and
specific. If someone says no or goes quiet after the final follow-up, stop. No made-up numbers, users or testimonials.
Each DM below is under 60 words.

## 11. The 15-minute interview script
Ask about the past, let them talk 80% of the time, write exact words. Pitch **only** at minute 12.
1. "Tell me about the last time a client paid late or didn't pay. What happened?"
2. "What did you do? How long did it take? How did you feel?"
3. "How do you invoice and collect today? Which tools, which apps?"
4. "How do you know a payment has actually reached your bank?"
5. "Has a client ever taken your work and stopped replying? What happened next?"
6. "What have you tried to fix this? Why didn't it stick?"
7. (Pitch, 2 minutes.) "I'm building a payment page where final files unlock when the client pays. On your last project, would that have helped? What would worry you?"
8. "Who else struggles with this? Could you introduce me?"
End by thanking them and sending the ₹200 UPI right away.
