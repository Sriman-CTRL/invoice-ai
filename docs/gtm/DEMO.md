# How to show Paivo

Demo pay page (works without signing in): `https://paivo.in` + the `DEMO_PAY_PATH` in `src/marketing.tsx`
(currently `/pay/13144558-e5f1-49ca-b968-8e7fe361d86e`). Use only fictional demo data on screen; never a real client's.
Screenshots from G3 (if generated) live in `docs/gtm/assets/`.

## 30-second pitch
"You know how you deliver the work and then the client goes quiet? With Paivo you send an invoice link. The client sees a UPI
QR for the exact amount, and your final files stay locked. They pay you directly, enter the transaction number, you check your
bank and tap Confirm, and the files unlock. We never touch the money, and there's no per-payment fee."

## 2-minute live demo
1. **Create an invoice (30 s).** Say: "Add the client, one line item, GST if you charge it, and paste the link to your final files."
2. **Share the link (15 s).** "This link goes on WhatsApp or email. It uses a random ID, so nobody can guess other invoices."
3. **Client pay page, files locked (30 s).** Open the demo pay page on a phone-sized window. "The client sees who is being paid, the exact amount, a UPI QR and a button to open their UPI app. Your files show as ready but locked."
4. **UTR (20 s).** "After paying they type the UTR, the transaction reference in their UPI app, and can attach a screenshot."
5. **Confirm and unlock (25 s).** Switch to the seller view. "You see the pending proof, match it in your bank app, and click confirm. Nothing changes until you do."
6. **Unlocked (10 s).** Refresh the client page: "Now the files link is visible. That's it."
Close with: "What would stop you from using this on your next invoice?" Then be quiet.

No claims about users, speed guarantees or compliance.

## Do not say
"Secure escrow" (we do not hold money), "automatic payment confirmation" (it is manual), "AI reads replies" (still "soon"),
"first in India" (unverified).
