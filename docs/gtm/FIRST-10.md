# First 10 users: the one-page plan

Built from [ICP.md](ICP.md), [CHANNELS.md](CHANNELS.md) and [MESSAGES.md](MESSAGES.md). Those files have the detail; this one
makes the choices so you can start today. Their rules still apply: one person at a time, say who you are, no bulk sending, and
**never write a real person's name, email, phone number or profile link in this repo**.

## 1. The one segment: freelance designers and video editors / motion artists in India

**Why this one first** (ICP.md ranks it #1):
- **The pain is exactly what Paivo fixes.** They hand over big final files, then the client goes quiet or says "will pay next week". Paivo holds the final-files link until payment is confirmed.
- **They already take UPI.** Most invoice with a Word/Canva/Docs file on WhatsApp and check the bank app by hand, so a pay page with a UPI QR is a small step, not a new habit.
- **Their work is public.** A portfolio or reel gives you a real, specific thing to mention in message one, which is what makes a cold message feel personal.
- **Fast to try.** One invoice takes under 10 minutes to set up, so "try it on one real invoice" is a fair ask.
- **Skip anyone** who only works through Fiverr or Upwork (the platform holds the money), is paid mostly from abroad by PayPal or Wise, or invoices once or twice a year.

Developers (#2) and small studios (#3) wait until you have talked to ten of these.

## 2. The 3 best places to find them

Check each place's rules and recent activity yourself before posting. I could not verify sizes or activity.

1. **LinkedIn post search** (best for people who just had a late payment). Search Posts, past month. These people said out loud that it hurts. Read the post, then message one person.
2. **Instagram and Behance** (best for people doing client work right now). Reels and project pages show what they make, so message one can name it.
3. **Freelancer communities: Freelance India Club (Discord / WhatsApp / LinkedIn) and Reddit r/FreelanceIndia.** Answer questions for a few days first. Then post a **question without a link** (message 6 in MESSAGES.md) and message only people who reply that it happens to them. Many communities ban promotion, so read the rules.

Do **not** solicit inside Fiverr or Upwork; it is against their terms. Warm contacts (friends, classmates, one intro each) come before all three; see [FIRST-10-USERS.md](../FIRST-10-USERS.md).

## 4. Ten messages to personalise

Each is under 60 words. Replace every `{slot}`. `{something they made}` must be a **real, specific** thing (a named reel, logo or
project). If you cannot name one, do not send the message. No link in the first message.

### 1. LinkedIn, after their late-payment post
- EN: `Hi {name}, sorry about the unpaid invoice. I also liked {something they made}. I'm {my name}, a solo builder researching how freelancers handle late payments. Not selling. Could I ask 3 quick questions (voice note works)? ₹200 UPI as thanks.`
- Hinglish: `Hi {name}, payment wali baat sunke bura laga. {something they made} bhi kaafi pasand aaya. Main {my name}, solo builder, freelancers ke late payments pe research kar raha hu. Kuch bech nahi raha. 3 chhote sawal pooch sakta hu (voice note chalega)? ₹200 UPI thanks.`

### 2. Instagram, from a reel
- EN: `Hi {name}! Loved {something they made}. I'm {my name}, building a tool for freelancers and researching how you chase late client payments. 3 quick questions, voice note is fine? ₹200 UPI from me as thanks.`
- Hinglish: `Hi {name}! {something they made} bahut accha laga. Main {my name}, freelancers ke liye tool bana raha hu aur late client payments pe research kar raha hu. 3 quick sawal, voice note chalega? Thanks me ₹200 UPI.`

### 3. Behance project
- EN: `Hi {name}, your project {something they made} is really well done. I'm {my name}, researching how designers get paid after delivery. Not a pitch. Could I ask 3 questions, 10 minutes or a voice note? ₹200 UPI thanks.`
- Hinglish: `Hi {name}, aapka {something they made} project bahut badhiya hai. Main {my name}, research kar raha hu ki designers delivery ke baad payment kaise lete hain. Pitch nahi hai. 3 sawal pooch sakta hu, 10 min ya voice note? ₹200 UPI thanks.`

### 4. X, after a "client not paying" post
- EN: `Hi {name}, saw your post about the pending payment. Sorry, that's frustrating. I enjoyed {something they made} too. I'm {my name}, researching this, not selling. What did you do next? 3 lines or a voice note is great. ₹200 UPI thanks.`
- Hinglish: `Hi {name}, pending payment wali post dekhi, sorry, frustrating hai. {something they made} bhi pasand aaya. Main {my name}, research kar raha hu, kuch bech nahi raha. Aapne aage kya kiya? 3 line ya voice note chalega. ₹200 UPI thanks.`

### 5. Community, after they replied to your question post
- EN: `Thanks for sharing that in the thread, {name}. I looked at {something they made}, nice work. I'm collecting stories like yours for a small project. Could I DM 3 questions? No link, no pitch. ₹200 UPI thanks.`
- Hinglish: `Thread mein batane ke liye thanks, {name}. {something they made} dekha, accha kaam hai. Aise kisse jama kar raha hu ek chhote project ke liye. 3 sawal DM kar sakta hu? Koi link ya pitch nahi. ₹200 UPI thanks.`

### 6. Warm: a friend who freelances
- EN: `Hey {name}! Remember {something they made}? That was great. I'm building a small tool for freelancers whose clients pay late. Can I ask you 3 questions about how you handle payments? 10 minutes, voice note is fine. Chai on me.`
- Hinglish: `Hey {name}! {something they made} yaad hai? Wo kaafi accha tha. Main freelancers ke liye ek chhota tool bana raha hu jinke clients late pay karte hain. Payments kaise handle karte ho, 3 sawal pooch sakta hu? 10 min, voice note chalega. Chai meri taraf se.`

### 7. Warm: intro request (to a friend or past colleague)
- EN: `Hi {name}, you know designers and editors. Is there one who had a client delay payment after delivery, maybe the person behind {something they made}? I'd love an intro. 15 minutes, no selling, UPI thanks to them.`
- Hinglish: `Hi {name}, aap designers aur editors ko jaante ho. Koi ek jiska client delivery ke baad payment late kar chuka ho, shayad {something they made} wale? Intro mil jaaye toh badhiya. 15 min, koi selling nahi, unhe UPI thanks.`

### 8. Reddit or Discord DM, after they answered a question
- EN: `Hi {name}, your answer about chasing clients was useful. I'm {my name}, a solo builder. I also saw {something they made}. Could I ask 3 follow-up questions here? Not selling. ₹200 UPI thanks if you're up for it.`
- Hinglish: `Hi {name}, clients ko chase karne wala aapka jawab kaam ka tha. Main {my name}, solo builder. {something they made} bhi dekha. 3 follow-up sawal yahin pooch sakta hu? Kuch bech nahi raha. Chaaho toh ₹200 UPI thanks.`

### 9. Cold email (public work email only; say who you are and how to opt out)
- EN: `Hi {name}, I'm {my name} in {city}. I came across {something they made} and wanted to ask 3 short questions about clients who pay late. Research, not selling; ₹200 UPI as thanks. Reply "no" and I won't write again.`
- Hinglish: `Hi {name}, main {my name}, {city} se. {something they made} dekha aur late paying clients pe 3 chhote sawal poochne the. Research hai, selling nahi; ₹200 UPI thanks. "no" reply kar do toh dobara nahi likhunga.`

### 10. Someone who said yes: booking the call
- EN: `Thanks for saying yes, {name}! Does {day} {time} work for 15 minutes, or would you rather send a voice note? I'd like to hear how {something they made} got paid. I'll send the ₹200 UPI right after.`
- Hinglish: `Haan bolne ke liye thanks, {name}! {day} {time} ko 15 min chalega, ya voice note behtar rahega? Sunna chahta hu {something they made} ki payment kaise hui. ₹200 UPI turant bhej dunga.`

Add a tagged link only after they ask, or at "try it on one real invoice", using the patterns in MESSAGES.md, for example
`?utm_source=linkedin&utm_medium=dm&utm_campaign=first10`.

## 5. Follow-up schedule (then stop)

| When | What | Template |
|---|---|---|
| Day 0 | First message (one of the ten above) | section 4 |
| Day 3, no reply | One light nudge | MESSAGES.md section 9 |
| Day 7, no reply | One last line | MESSAGES.md section 9 |
| After day 7 | Close politely; do not write again unless they do | MESSAGES.md section 9 (final close) |
| They say yes | Book within a day; run the 15-minute script, pitch only at minute 12 | message 10, MESSAGES.md section 11 |
| Right after the call | Send the ₹200 UPI the same day | n/a |
| Day 1 after the call | Ask them to try one real invoice (offer a 10-minute setup call) | MESSAGES.md section 12 |
| After their first invoice | Ask what was most confusing | MESSAGES.md section 13 |
| After it worked once | Ask for one referral | MESSAGES.md section 14 |

A "no", or someone who looks distressed or in a legal dispute, ends the thread immediately. Do not follow up.

## 6. What to track in outreach-tracker.csv

One row per message sent. The columns are already in the file:
`date, channel, segment, source_link, message_template_used, replied, interview, tried_product, notes`.

- `date`: day of the first message.
- `channel`: linkedin, instagram, behance, x, reddit, discord, email, whatsapp or intro.
- `segment`: `design-video` for everyone in this plan, so later segments stay separate.
- `source_link`: the link to the **community or kind of post**, never a person's profile.
- `message_template_used`: the number from section 4 plus language (`1-EN`, `2-HI`), so you learn which message and language work.
- `replied`: `no`, `yes` or `declined`. Put the day of the reply in `notes` ("d2") to see how fast people answer.
- `interview`: `no`, `booked` or `done`.
- `tried_product`: `no`, `setup`, `first-invoice` or `paid-via-paivo`.
- `notes`: **no names, emails, phone numbers or handles.** Write the pain in their own words ("client vanished after final files") and anything that surprised you.

Review once a week:
1. Messages sent, replies, interviews done and people who tried it. Compare reply rate by channel and by English vs Hinglish.
2. The three most repeated pain phrases.
3. The decision rule from [FIRST-10-USERS.md](../FIRST-10-USERS.md): if 5 of 10 people say they would use it this month, build more; if they shrug, change the idea, not the code. That doc also quotes a benchmark of roughly 30-70 names per customer for cold outreach (a rough guide, not a promise), so build a list of 50 or more before you start.

Paivo records `utm_*` and `ref` tags, so sign-ups by source show in the admin Growth page; match them to the tracker.
