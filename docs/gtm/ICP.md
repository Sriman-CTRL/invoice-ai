# Who to talk to first (ICP)

Extends [FIRST-10-USERS.md](../FIRST-10-USERS.md). Pick **one** segment for the first 14 days; do not split effort.

## Market size, honestly
There is no official count of freelancers in India. Government numbers measure gig and platform workers, a different group:
NITI Aayog's 2022 report estimated about 7.7 million gig workers in 2020-21 and projected 23.5 million by 2029-30
([IndBiz](https://indbiz.gov.in/indias-gig-economy-to-have-23-5-million-workers-by-2029-30-niti-aayog/));
the Economic Survey 2025-26 headline is about 12 million, roughly 2% of the workforce
([Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/union-budget/story/india-has-12-million-gig-workers-2-of-total-workforce-economic-survey-513429-2026-01-29.html)).
Don't quote any of these as "number of freelancers". For Paivo the market is a few thousand reachable conversations,
not millions.

## Ranked segments

### 1. Freelance designers and video editors / motion artists (start here)
- **Payment pain:** deliver big files, client goes quiet or "will pay next week"; final files already handed over.
- **How they invoice today:** a Word/Canva/Google Docs invoice sent on WhatsApp, or a free Zoho/Refrens account; payment by UPI to a personal ID; checking the bank app by hand.
- **Where they hang out:** Instagram and Behance portfolios, Dribbble, LinkedIn, Reddit/Discord freelance groups, WhatsApp groups of editors.
- **What would make them switch:** one link that shows the QR, hides the final file until paid, and shows who owes what. Must take under 10 minutes to try.
- **Red flags:** they only work through Fiverr/Upwork (platform holds the money, so no direct invoicing); clients are mostly abroad paying by PayPal/Wise (UPI is irrelevant); they only invoice once or twice a year.

### 2. Freelance developers and no-code builders with 3+ clients
- **Pain:** milestone payments, repo or deployment handed over before the final payment, GST questions.
- **Today:** Zoho/Refrens or spreadsheets, bank transfers to a current account.
- **Hang out:** LinkedIn, X, GitHub, Discord, college alumni groups, indie-builder communities.
- **Switch trigger:** unlock of a repo/Drive/Figma link after payment; GST invoice with GSTIN; recurring retainer invoices.
- **Red flags:** they already have a polished invoicing setup with an accountant; clients pay by company bank transfer with a 45-day cycle (the lock adds nothing).

### 3. Small studios (2-10 people) and consultants/tutors who deliver files
- **Pain:** several people chase payments; partial payments; TDS deducted by corporate clients.
- **Today:** Zoho Books/Tally with an accountant, plus WhatsApp chasing.
- **Hang out:** LinkedIn, local business groups, co-working spaces.
- **Switch trigger:** TDS handling and proper PDFs for finance teams; partial payments.
- **Red flags:** longer decision cycle, accountant is the buyer, wants e-invoicing/GSTR filing that Paivo does not do.

## What to ask in every segment
Use the past-behaviour script in [MESSAGES.md](MESSAGES.md). The question that sorts good from bad fits: "Tell me about the
last time a client paid late. How did you find out whether the money had arrived?"
