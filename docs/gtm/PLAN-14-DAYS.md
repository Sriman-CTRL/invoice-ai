# 14-day plan to talk to the first users

Goal: 10 real conversations and 3 people trying Paivo on a real invoice. Log every contact in
[outreach-tracker.csv](outreach-tracker.csv) (no personal data). Pick ONE segment from [ICP.md](ICP.md) (suggested: freelance
designers/video editors).

| Day | Do | Daily target |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Read ICP and MESSAGES. Set up the tracker. List 20 warm contacts (in your head or on paper, not in the repo). Join 3 communities from CHANNELS and read, don't post. | 20 warm contacts listed |
| 2 | Send template 1 to 10 warm contacts, one by one. | 10 messages |
| 3 | Send template 1 to the other 10. Post 1 question (template 6) in one allowed community. | 10 messages, 1 post |
| 4 | Reply to everyone. Book interviews. Ask 5 people for intros (template 2). | 2 conversations |
| 5 | Run interviews (script in MESSAGES). Send ₹200 thanks right after. | 2-3 conversations |
| 6 | Send 8 cold DMs (templates 3-5) using search queries in CHANNELS. | 8 messages |
| 7 | Follow-up day 3 for day-4 sends. Review notes; write down the top 3 pains you heard. | 3 follow-ups |
| 8 | 8 more cold DMs. Answer 3 questions in communities (template 7 replies). | 8 messages, 3 replies |
| 9 | Interviews. Offer "try on one real invoice" (template 12) to those who confirm the pain. | 2 conversations |
| 10 | 8 more DMs; help anyone trying the product set up on a call. | 8 messages |
| 11 | Day-7 follow-ups. Post one build-in-public update (CONTENT.md). | follow-ups, 1 post |
| 12 | Interviews and set-up calls. Ask for feedback (template 13). | 2 conversations |
| 13 | Write up patterns: how many felt the pain, who tried, who paid attention to file-lock. | summary written |
| 14 | Decide using the rule below. Thank everyone who helped. | decision made |

**Measure daily:** messages sent, replies, interviews held, people who tried the product, and where sign-ups came from (the admin Growth page shows sources once G2 is live).

## Decision rule on day 14
- **5 or more of 10** say they would use it this month and 3+ tried it on a real invoice → double down: build what they named as the top pain.
- **Pain is real but not the file lock** (e.g. chasing, knowing money arrived) → keep the product, change what you build next (see FIRST-10-USERS.md section 7).
- **Most shrug, or "my clients pay fine"** → narrow to a different segment for 14 more days, or change the idea. Don't write more code first.
- **Fewer than 10 conversations happened** → the decision is not valid; extend by a week and fix outreach, not the product.
