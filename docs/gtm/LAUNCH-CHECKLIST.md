# Launch checklist

Items marked **(owner login)** need your accounts. Do them after the SEO work (T1) is merged and deployed.

## Search engines
- [ ] **Google Search Console (owner login):** add the `paivo.in` property (DNS TXT verification is the most durable method). Submit `https://paivo.in/sitemap.xml`. Then use URL inspection on `/`, `/pricing`. https://search.google.com/search-console
- [ ] **Bing Webmaster Tools (owner login):** import the property from Search Console, or add the site manually and submit the sitemap. https://www.bing.com/webmasters
- [ ] After deploy, check `https://paivo.in/robots.txt`, `/sitemap.xml`, `/og.png` load. Paste the home URL into a WhatsApp chat and a LinkedIn post preview to check the card.

## Business listings
- [ ] **Google Business Profile:** only applies if you have a public address or serve a defined area; a purely online product often does not qualify. Skip unless you have such an address. https://www.google.com/business/

## Launch directories (check each site's current rules before posting)
| Directory | Link | Needs |
|---|---|---|
| Product Hunt | https://www.producthunt.com | Owner account; launch page with tagline, images, first comment. Best after the demo screenshots exist. |
| Indie Hackers | https://www.indiehackers.com | Account; create a product page and post a build-in-public update. |
| BetaList | https://betalist.com | Submission form, may involve a fee or queue for free listings (check). |
| Indian startup directories and communities | Search "list your startup India" | Check legitimacy and cost before submitting; avoid paid link farms. |
| Alternative-to sites (e.g. AlternativeTo) | https://alternativeto.net | Account; list as an alternative to invoicing tools without claiming rankings. |

## Before sharing publicly
- [ ] Landing, pricing and privacy pages read correctly on a phone.
- [ ] Test sign-up end to end, and the demo pay page.
- [ ] `[soon]` labels still match reality (automatic reminders and AI replies are not live).
- [ ] A support email that you read: `VITE_SUPPORT_EMAIL`.
- [ ] Tagged links ready per channel (see MESSAGES.md).
