# Where to find first users (public places only)

**Rules for you:** one-to-one, personal, honest about who you are. No mass messaging, no scraping, no lists of people.
Never record names, phone numbers, emails or profile URLs in this repo. Verify every community's rules and recent activity
yourself before posting; **I could not open most of these pages overnight, so sizes below are only what third-party
listings reported, and "recent activity" is not checked.** Mark each channel in the tracker after you look.

## Search queries (paste into the site's own search)

Use these to find people who **publicly** talked about late payments recently. Read the post, then send one personal message
(see [MESSAGES.md](MESSAGES.md)). Don't contact anyone who seems distressed or whose post is about a legal dispute.

- **Google:** `site:linkedin.com/posts "client" "not paid" freelancer India`  ·  `site:reddit.com freelance India client not paying invoice`  ·  `"late payment" freelancer India UPI invoice`
- **LinkedIn search (Posts, past month):** `"client hasn't paid" freelancer`  ·  `"payment pending" "freelance" India`  ·  `"ghosted" client invoice designer`
- **X search:** `"client not paying" freelancer lang:en`  ·  `"still waiting for payment" (designer OR editor OR developer)`  ·  `"payment pending" freelancer india`
- **Instagram:** hashtags `#freelancerlife #freelancedesigner #videoeditorindia #freelanceindia`; look for stories/posts about unpaid work.
